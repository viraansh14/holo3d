HOLOROOM walk inside it

Head-tracked window into a 3D world. No glasses.
How to feel the 3D:
1. Mouse mode — move the cursor; the world shifts with real parallax, like looking through a window.
2. Webcam mode — it tracks your head; lean side to side and look around the floating objects. This is the magic one.
3. Set Screen size to your actual display for correct perspective.
4. Project this fullscreen onto a wall → the wall becomes a window.
5. Got a VR/AR headset? Hit Enter XR and step fully inside.
Tip: a dark room makes the void disappear and the hologram float.
